Dev Runner now checks that your services actually answer, and you can change a project without stopping it.

## New

- **Health checks.** Dev Runner no longer waits for one exact line in the log. By default, a service counts as running as soon as its port answers or it prints its usual startup line, so apps with their own logging no longer show "Running? (no ready line)".
- You can pick the check for each service in **Edit**: its port, a web address such as `/health`, a line in the log, or nothing at all.
- **It keeps checking.** If a running service stops answering, its card says **Not responding** and why, and goes back to Running when it answers again. Nothing is stopped or restarted because of it.
- **Edit while it runs.** You no longer have to stop a project to edit it. When you save, only the services you changed restart.
- **Edit a single service** straight from its card with the new pencil button.

## Better

- The project editor is grouped into the service, its address and health check, and how it starts.
- The `.dev.json` guide explains the health checks. Old configs with `readyPattern` keep working.
